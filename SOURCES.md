# Sources

Every medical number, timing, threshold, and call-the-doctor rule in `app.js`
traces to one of these pages. Each was opened and quoted in September 2026.
If a claim cannot be traced here, it does not belong in the app.

## Emergencies and call-now rules
- Fever under 3 months: AAP, Fever and Your Baby. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/fever/Pages/Fever-and-Your-Baby.aspx
- Breathing pauses over 10 seconds: Cleveland Clinic, Periodic Breathing. https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/periodic-breathing
- 911 list (blue/gray/pale/blotchy, floppy, seizure, rash that does not fade): NHS, Soothing a crying baby. https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/soothing-a-crying-baby/
- Major bleeding, blue lips, breathing trouble: Seattle Children's, Emergency Symptoms Not to Miss. https://www.seattlechildrens.org/conditions/a-z/emergency-symptoms-not-to-miss/
- Falls: KidsHealth, Falls First Aid. https://kidshealth.org/en/parents/falls-sheet.html
- Crying over 2 hours: AAP Symptom Checker, Crying Baby Before 3 Months. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/tips-tools/symptom-checker/Pages/symptomviewer.aspx?symptom=Crying+Baby+-+Before+3+Months+Old
- Blood in stool: AAP Symptom Checker, Stools Blood In. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/tips-tools/symptom-checker/Pages/symptomviewer.aspx?symptom=Stools+-+Blood+In
- Jaundice: AAP, Jaundice in Newborns. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/Pages/Jaundice.aspx
- Cord stump (3 weeks): AAP, Umbilical Cord Care. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/bathing-skin-care/Pages/Umbilical-Cord-Care.aspx
- Circumcision (8 hours, 10 days): KidsHealth, Circumcision. https://kidshealth.org/en/parents/circumcision.html
- Newborn rashes: AAP Symptom Checker, Newborn Rashes. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/tips-tools/symptom-checker/Pages/symptomviewer.aspx?symptom=Newborn+Rashes+and+Birthmarks
- Cradle cap: AAP. https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/bathing-skin-care/Pages/Cradle-Cap.aspx
- Peeling skin: Cleveland Clinic. https://health.clevelandclinic.org/newborn-skin-peeling

## Her

- Urgent maternal warning signs, tell them she was recently pregnant: CDC Hear Her. https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html
- Postpartum psychosis is an emergency, 911 or ER: NIMH, Perinatal Depression. https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/perinatal-depression
- Maternal Mental Health Hotline 1-833-852-6262, 988: CDC, Depression Resources. https://www.cdc.gov/reproductive-health/depression/resources.html
